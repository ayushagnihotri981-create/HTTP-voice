# ZentroMart Store Policies (Knowledge Base)

## 1. Grocery Return & Replacement Policy
- Fresh fruits, vegetables, dairy, and baked goods can only be returned or reported within **2 hours** of delivery if spoiled or damaged.
- Packaged foods (pulses, grains, snacks) have a **24-hour return window** provided packaging is unopened.
- Proof of damage/spoilage (photo upload) is required for refund processing.

## 2. Electronics Return & Warranty Policy
- All electronics (televisions, soundbars, headphones, accessories) have a **7-day replacement/return window** starting from the delivery timestamp.
- Returned electronics must include the original retail box, accessories, warranty cards, and intact serial numbers.
- Products damaged by electrical surge or customer negligence are not covered under store warranty.

## 3. Order Cancellation & Modifications
- Orders can be freely cancelled prior to the "Dispatched" status directly via the mobile app or support.
- Once dispatched, orders cannot be cancelled mid-transit; customers may refuse delivery at their doorstep.

## 4. Refund Methods and Timelines
- **UPI Payments**: Refunds reflect in customer bank accounts within **24 to 48 hours**.
- **Credit / Debit Cards**: Refunds process within **5 to 7 business days**.
- **ZentroMart Wallet**: Instant credit upon approval.

## 5. Customer Care Contacts & Operating Hours
- Helpline: **1800-ZENTRO (1800-936-876)** (Available 24x7)
- Email: **support@zentromart.in**
- Live Chat: Available in the ZentroMart App (7:00 AM - 11:00 PM IST)
